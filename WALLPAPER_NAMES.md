## 📊 Naming Convention

**Format**: `[Number]-[Descriptive-Name].jpg`

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Numbers control display order (01, 02, 03...)
- ✅ Descriptive names are clear and appealing
- ✅ Hyphens convert to spaces in UI
- ✅ Professional and clean appearance
- ✅ Easy to add more wallpapers in sequence

## 🔄 Adding New Wallpapers

To add more wallpapers, follow the naming pattern:
```
07-Your-Wallpaper-Name.jpg
08-Another-Wallpaper.jpg
09-Third-Wallpaper.jpg
```

The numbers ensure they appear in the order you want.
