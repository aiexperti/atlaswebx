The AI cockpit for your web

AtlaswebX is the free, open-source AI browser built for people who want an alternative to closed copilots like ChatGPT desktop, Atlas Browser, Perplexity, Arc, and Opera GX. Enjoy a Tesla-inspired cockpit that safeguards your privacy while giving you full control to run ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, self-hosted, or local models side by side—without ever leaving your tab.

Connected AI Engines
ChatGPT Claude DeepSeek Gemini More

Multi-AI freedom. Single cockpit.

Route prompts to ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, or your own self-hosted models with one keystroke. AtlaswebX keeps every provider under your control—without surrendering data to walled-garden browsers.

Unified AI Sidebar

Pin multiple copilots side by side, retain full conversation history, and launch workflows right from the page you are reading.

Instant Model Switching

Jump across GPT-4o, Claude 3, DeepSeek R1, Gemini 1.5, local LLMs, or on-prem clusters—no lock-in, no tabs to shuffle.

Secure Key Vault

Your keys never leave your device. Encrypted local storage, zero telemetry, and optional offline mode keep corporate copilots out of your logs.
